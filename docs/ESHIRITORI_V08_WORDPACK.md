# DRAWINDOW v0.8 picture-shiritori expansion

**Stacked Draft review only**. Based on the user's reference: https://kotoba-moji.com/eshiritori-kotoba/. Vocabulary ideas are curated independently; no source illustrations or art paths were copied.

14 original hand-authored black-line doodles, with the same strokes mapped to phonetic Japanese and single-word English labels:

| Theme | Japanese | English | Provisional SOFT caveats |
|---|---|---|---|
| DAY | すべりだい, ひよこ, さくらんぼ, あり | slide, chick, cherry, ant | chick vs bird |
| NIGHT | かぼちゃ, ほたる, しろ, ほね | pumpkin, firefly, castle, bone | firefly vs insect |
| COMMON | いす, えび, はこ, くり, るびー, にじ | chair, shrimp, box, chestnut, ruby, rainbow | shrimp, chestnut, gemstone, monochrome rainbow |

"るびー" provides another る exit beyond るーれっと; "にじ" gives に a nonterminal candidate instead of only にんじん. No additions end in ん. DAY/NIGHT are *soft weighting*, not exclusive vocabularies.

All SAFE/SOFT ratings are provisional internal ratings, **not measured first-look recognition**. At 360px size, gather 3–5 blind first answers, then separate Shizuku-style charm ratings. Test EN/JA x DAY/NIGHT in a real Android session and verify LIVE recognition separately from DEMO.

This branch does not replace the older nine bilingual drawings, including the user's locally revised Shizuku line v0.2. Do not merge to main, deploy, or publish without approval.
