DRAWINDOW v0.2

Character scene and expressions:
OpenAI built-in ImageGen, generated 2026-10-17 from the user-supplied daylight
reference. The final scene is generated illustration. Source atlas, prompt,
processing steps and SHA-256 hashes are retained in the source project.
No third-party character artwork was downloaded or traced.

Girl's drawing templates:
Contains 23 selected drawings from The Quick, Draw! Dataset, made available
by Google, Inc. / Google Creative Lab under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International (CC BY 4.0).
Dataset: https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: source vector coordinates normalized to 0..1, cropped to a square
playback region; synthetic timestamps added for pen-stroke replay.
Individual original drawing IDs and exact source category URLs are retained
in src/data/sketch-provenance.json in the source project.
Queen, xylophone and yarn are original DRAWINDOW stroke templates.
Dataset recognition tags are historical source metadata, not evidence about
this game's recognition API.

Fonts:
DM Sans - The DM Sans Project Authors.
Cormorant Garamond - The Cormorant Project Authors.
SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full licenses are in licenses/.
Icons: Phosphor Icons, MIT. https://phosphoricons.com/

DEMO is explicitly simulated and performs no image recognition.
No audio recording is used; optional quiet sounds are synthesized locally.
